# Metropoliten

A first-person psychological horror game built in Unity.

A desperate man takes a job as a trainee metro driver in a poor, fictional Bulgarian-adjacent country whose metro shouldn't plausibly exist, and slowly realizes he wasn't hired by accident.

## Status

Early development. The Opening scene and the first two shifts are playable, from the main menu through the registry and a night's service with passengers to the end of the line, with music and sound throughout. The game saves as you go, so Continue picks up where you left off, part way through a shift.

## Running it

1. Open the project in Unity 6000.6.7f1.
2. Open `Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity` and press Play.

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD |
| Look | Mouse |
| Sprint | Left Shift |
| Talk, continue | E |
| Choose a reply | W / S then E, or number keys |
| Drive the train | W for power, S for the brake |
| Open and close the train doors | E, from the seat |
| Hand lamp | F |
| Pause, settings | Esc |

## Working on it

- Each scene's dialogue is in `Assets/Dialogue`. `Opening.txt` explains the format.
- The scenes are generated by the scripts in `Assets/Editor`. **Metropoliten > Build Opening Scene**, **Build Shift 1 Scene**, **Build Shift 2 Scene** and **Build Main Menu Scene** rebuild them and rebake the lighting, replacing any hand edits to the scene.
- People are generated as well. Everyone's look is written down in `Assets/Editor/Characters/Cast.cs`, and `CharacterForge` builds their body, face and clothes around a shared skeleton. **CastPhoto.Run** photographs the whole cast to check a change.
- The town at the end of Shift 2 and its fog are built with the scene. The fog is a full-screen pass, `Assets/Shaders/OpenFog.shader`, and the photograph in the locker is rendered by `PhotoStudio` from a small set it builds for the purpose and throws away.
- All of the music and sound is generated too, by `Assets/Editor/Sound`. **Metropoliten > Forge Sounds** writes it to `Assets/Resources/Sound`. A line like `@ S_Cold` in a dialogue file plays one of those sounds at that point in the conversation.
