# Everything said on the platform in Shift 1. A first draft: change anything you like.
# The format is explained at the top of Opening.txt.
# A conversation carries on past labels until it reaches "-> end", so every one needs an "-> end".
#
# The scene itself uses the labels briefing, intercom_again, log, log_again, key, key_again,
# signal, signal_again, cab_locked and seat. On the run it uses drive_moving, drive_fast,
# drive_station and drive_short, which each show one line while the player drives,
# then drive_stopped or drive_overran once the train comes to rest.

= briefing
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: There you are. Right on time.
DISPATCHER: I'm the dispatcher. You'll hear me on this box and on the cab radio.
> Where is everyone?
> The clerk said you'd have instructions. -> instructions
DISPATCHER: Night shift. There's you, and there's me.

= instructions
DISPATCHER: Tonight is easy. Three things before you take the train.
DISPATCHER: Sign the log at the duty booth. Take the cab key from the board. Check the signal at the end of the platform.
DISPATCHER: Then get in the cab and I'll talk you through the rest.
> Understood.
> That's all?
DISPATCHER: One more thing. There's bread in the booth. Eat something.
DISPATCHER: Nobody's cooking for you this month.
> ...Thanks.
> [Say nothing]
DISPATCHER: Good lad. Go on.
-> end

= intercom_again
NIKOLA: Nothing. Just the hum on the line.
-> end

= log
@ I_Pen
NIKOLA: Name. Time. Signature.
-> end

= log_again
NIKOLA: Already signed.
-> end

= key
@ I_Key
NIKOLA: Hook two. Cab key.
-> end

= key_again
NIKOLA: Got what I came for.
-> end

= signal
NIKOLA: Green. Tunnel's clear.
-> end

= signal_again
NIKOLA: Still green.
-> end

= cab_locked
NIKOLA: Locked. I'm not done on the platform.

= seat
@ I_Seat
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: Good. That's your seat from now on.
DISPATCHER: One run tonight. Severna, one stop up the line.
DISPATCHER: Lever forward for power, back for the brake. Stop with your nose at the board on the platform.
> And if I go past it?
> Understood. -> away
DISPATCHER: You won't. The train doesn't let anyone go far.

= away
DISPATCHER: Take her out slowly.
-> end

= drive_moving
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: That's it. Nice and easy.

= drive_fast
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: Ease off. Fifty is plenty down here.

= drive_station
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: Severna's coming up. Start bringing her down.

= drive_short
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: You're short of the board. Bring her up a little.

= drive_stopped
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: There. That's a stop.
DISPATCHER: Leave the key in the cab. Someone will see to the train.
> Who? There's nobody down here.
> [Say nothing] -> goodnight
DISPATCHER: Someone always sees to it.

= goodnight
@ S_Cold
DISPATCHER: Go home, Nikola. Same time tomorrow.
-> end

= drive_overran
@ I_Radio
DISPATCHER: That's past the board. The train stopped itself, not you.
DISPATCHER: Everybody does it once.
-> goodnight
